## Composition and accuracy

Production tool: built-in `imagegen`, using each supplied licensed reference. Brief: photo-first 2:3 composition, retain major geography, navy/cream text hierarchy, short exact title and CTA, PortdayGuide.com branding, no invented fares or route maps. Cozumel copy: “COZUMEL TAXI COSTS” / “Plan your beach-day budget” / “Compare the whole-trip cost”. Yokohama copy: “TOKYO TO YOKOHAMA CRUISE PORT” / “Train, taxi or private transfer?” / “Free departure checklist”. Final PNGs are 1000 × 1500.

The two covers are photo-based, AI-assisted re-rendered compositions made from the licensed source photographs, with text and branding. Visual details vary from the source. They must not be described as unaltered real photographs or as literal photo crops. Each new feed description says “Photo-based illustration.” The source credits identify the underlying photographs, not photographer authorship or approval of the finished compositions.

Use the images as promotional scenery only. They do not verify a named beach club, terminal entrance, pickup point, walking route, real-time conditions or travel time. Guide copy and official sources remain the basis for those decisions. Future scenic heroes should prefer licensed real photographs with short readable text, following the owner's September 30 direction.
